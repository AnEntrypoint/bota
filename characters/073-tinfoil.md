# 073 Tinfoil

## Card

- Role family: Drifter
- Role: Ranged nuker
- Attribute: Agi
- Range: Ranged
- Distinct prop: Copper wire coat-hanger antenna as a wand
- Value groups (body / head / prop): M / L / D

## Subject

suit made entirely of crumpled overlapping sheets of aluminum foil, tall conical shiny foil wizard hat, twisted copper wire coat-hanger antenna held like a wand, tense defensive crouch

## Hook

Strikes from behind while unseen, blinks with a coat hanger, and leaves foil balls that revive allies once.
