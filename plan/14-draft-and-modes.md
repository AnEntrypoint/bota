# 14. Draft and modes
The draft is the first decision of the match. It sets the hero pool, the lane plan and the item plan.

- Job: draft (plan/02, Draft row). The cost is the hero the other team takes first.

### Game modes

| Mode | Use | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Showrunner Draft | Ranked and main | Two captains (the first seated player on each team, UNVERIFIED) ban and pick in turn. 30 s per pick, 130 s bonus time per team (UNVERIFIED). |
| Open Market | Casual | Each player picks freely. No bans. |
| Demand Draft | Casual | Heroes drawn from a 50-hero pool (UNVERIFIED). |
| Shuffle Draft | Casual | Each player picks from a random 10-hero set (UNVERIFIED). |
| Ranked Open Market | Ranked | Each player nominates a hero, then half the nominated heroes are banned at random. Then the remaining heroes are picked in turn (order UNVERIFIED); a banned nominee is lost to its nominator, so nominating costs a pick. |
| Short Form | Casual or ranked, 25 minute target | Two lanes per side, mid removed. Trend opens at 5:00. Exchange falls once both enemy T2s, one in each lane, are down. Surrender vote at 15 min, overriding the 30 min rule in section 2 (UNVERIFIED). UNVERIFIED |

### Showrunner Draft order

Ten bans and ten picks, in the order below (1st and 2nd name the two teams). The order (final order):

1. Ban: 1st, 2nd, 1st, 2nd
2. Pick: 1st, 2nd, 2nd, 1st
3. Ban: 2nd, 1st, 2nd, 1st
4. Pick: 2nd, 1st, 2nd, 1st
5. Ban: 2nd, 1st
6. Pick: 1st, 2nd

### Short Form

Short Form is the same game on a smaller map. All numbers are UNVERIFIED.

- Job: map pressure and rotation (plan/02, Map pressure, rotation row). The cost is the late-game rotations this mode gives up.
- Two lanes per side (top and bottom). Mid lane and its tower and Content Mill rows are removed.
- Position 2 takes a surviving lane alone (top or bottom, UNVERIFIED), since mid is removed.
- Neutral items: tiers 1 and 2 only. Tiers 3 and 4 start at 27:00 and 37:00, past the 25 minute target (UNVERIFIED).
- The Trend opens at 5:00 and respawns in 4 to 6 min (a Short Form override of plan/12's 8 to 11 min, UNVERIFIED).
- Exchange is invulnerable until both enemy Tier 2 towers, one in each lane, fall, not both Tier 4s.
- The XP a hero gains is multiplied by 1.5; the XP needed per level is unchanged (UNVERIFIED).
- Clout gain, Reach, Stan and the Resell chain work as in the full game. Breaking News runs its 18:00 event only (UNVERIFIED).

### Roles

| Position | Role | Lane | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Carry | Safe lane | Clout carry or late-game damage |
| 2 | Mid | Mid | Highest-impact solo lane |
| 3 | Offlane | Off lane | Initiator, tank or disruptor |
| 4 | Roamer | Safe lane, with carry | Soft support, gank pressure |
| 5 | Hard support | Safe lane, with carry | Vision, protection, Stan support |

Jungler is a viable role for heroes with camp-clearing kits (Weegee, Offline sustain, Assassin, vision denial archetype). A jungler is a position choice, not a sixth slot.

### Archetype lanes

Lane preference by archetype. This is guidance for the draft UI, not a restriction.

- Carry: Late-game Clout carry, Clout carry, Ranged nuker
- Mid: Ranged nuker, Ghost-account summoner, Hot-take initiator
- Offlane: Reach tank, initiator, Hot-take initiator
- Roamer: Assassin, vision denial, Harasser, zoner, Tethered support, dueler
- Hard support: Resell healer, buffer, Utility, zone control

### Ban phase

- Bans are hidden until the phase ends, then revealed together.
- A banned hero cannot be picked by either team for the match.
- Pick and ban rates are tracked for balance (section 19).

### Pick screen

The pick screen is titled "Draft your content plan." It shows the archetype role icon on every hero, and the Trend modifier for its 4 min once it is known.
