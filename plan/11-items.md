# 11. Items
### Inventory

Numbers in this section are UNVERIFIED.

- Six item slots, plus a six-slot stash. Backpack: three slots, see section 21.
- Consumables stack. Stock-limited items have a restock timer (Lurker Cam, Smoke of Deniability).
- Sell-back: full refund within 10 s of purchase, 50% after that. The Trend's Snapshot (section 12) and Coffee (section 21) cannot be sold.
- Drop-on-death items: Hot Take, Unironic and Gem of Subtext. Their holder drops them on death, visible to all.
- Items that take an active use a shared item cooldown per item, not per hero.
- Delivery Drone: the flying courier that an Envelope item sends to base. Its speed bonus is the Envelope component's.

### Shop

- The Spawn Lounge shop is run by The Plug, the shop NPC (section 18). It sells every component, every Tier 1 finished item and the consumable wards.
- The Back Alley Shop sells the Tier 2, Tier 3 and Tier 4 finished items. The two Lane Stalls sell components only (section 4).
- Purchases spend reliable gold first, then unreliable gold.

Design notes:

- Anon Mask is an invisibility item. It is not a blink, and the name matches the function.
- Pro Plan is named for what it does.
- Orb effects do not stack (plan/20 decision 3). Other attack modifiers stack additively: percent bonuses add together, then flat bonuses add (UNVERIFIED).
