# 15. Roster and role families

112 heroes across twelve gameplay role families. Roster slots 001 to 112 are characters 001 to 112 from bota/characters (INDEX.tsv order). Reserve 113 to 117 (Wanderer, Raker, Seeder, Wartrucker, Bouncer) has no role family or attribute until promoted. Each row gives the character, role family, attribute, range and a signature gag taken from the character prompt. The old archetype names are kept in the last column of the family table only.

Each variant differs from the others in at least two of: body proportion, prop silhouette, primary value group. A Stalker in a hood and a Stalker in a hazmat suit share a role family and read as two heroes.

Rules for the roster columns:

- **Attr.** Within each role family, in roster order, attribute cycles Str, Agi, Int. Every family therefore holds all three, and the overall split is about one third each.
- **Range.** Melee is unarmed or a hand-held close weapon or prop. Ranged is a projectile, beam, sound, throw or thrown-out prop. Where the prompt gives no weapon, range defaults to Melee.
