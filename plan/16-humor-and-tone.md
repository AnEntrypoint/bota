# 16. Humor and tone
The register is maximalist, meme-forward and then some. Two images carry two readings. The second reading is the punchline.

### Rules
1. Jokes target observed behavior, each anchored to a character: Boomer follows you since the lobby (GUESS), Trollhunter bumps old threads (GUESS), Pointing points at everything.
2. Every joke has a setup and a punchline. Harold's WORLD'S BEST mug is the setup; the pained grimace is the punchline.
3. Voice is expressive and reactive. Rageguy and Krazed scream; Viking reacts by scrolling past. Only the Burst initiator screams.
4. Gags have a lifespan. Forever replays the same loop, Acorn rebrands every week (GUESS), Krazed's keyboard breaks once and is folded into the next patch.
5. Meme vocabulary is original to the roster: Smugcat's squint carries cope, Viking's Load more carries the Trend, Npc repeats one line (GUESS). Phrases rotate per patch.
6. Loaded phrases (cope, seethe, mald, skill issue, cry about it, L + ratio, ok boomer, learn to code) are voiced by Smugcat and Rageguy and aim at the play, a kill, a death or a lane, never at a person.
7. Hillarity is the goal. Relevance is the constraint. Tpose floats with arms locked out and lands; Npc's repeat is cut unless it lands once.
