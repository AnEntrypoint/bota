# 16. Humor and tone
The register is maximalist, meme-literate and visually prosecutable: if a joke names a prop, the prop is in the gallery JPG. The comedy is a three-chain sitcom rather than a meme-name checklist. The **Receipt Chain** bills reality (Neckbeard's crumb invoice, Boomer's grass-stain bill, Howard's incident report). The **Repost Chain** refuses to let anything end (Grey's final final v3 memo, Npc's weather patch note, Forever's clip, Gothgirl's pre-vibe rewind). The **Camera Chain** stages the same argument three ways (Bot Bernard dodges the portrait, Noscope brings orbit-grade optics to a no-scope, Shoop wipes between takes). Ecce captions every recap Behold, again.

### Rules
1. A joke must have a setup, a visible detail, a game state and a punchline. Boomer follows you since the lobby because the black shades and silver can are already in the replay (GUESS).
2. References are layered, not listed. A monkey dodges its own portrait while a sniper over-equips a no-scope; that is the camera, the vanity and the scoreboard all at once, without naming a franchise.
3. Voice is expressive and reactive. A hero reacts by scrolling past. Only the Hot-take initiator screams; Gothgirl is allergic to screaming.
4. Running gags escalate exactly three times: introduce, misfile, appeal. The Receipt Chain ends with Howard dating every prior document; the Repost Chain ends before the word vibe; the Camera Chain ends with a transition wipe that erases the cameraman.
5. Fad vocabulary is the roster's own Trend and Clout terms. Npc repeats one line (GUESS); phrases rotate per patch. Two dated platform fads are kept on purpose: Nobody: / Me: (gag 18) and [deleted] (gag 11).
6. The stock phrases (cope, skill issue, L + markdown, We're so back., F., Cooked.) remain announcer-only and aim at the play, never a person.
7. Every announcer line names the game state it reacts to. Npc's repeat is cut unless it lands once.
8. No retired or original-game character name appears in a shipped joke. Slot 003 is a retired visual reference only: the monochrome statue has no kit, line or appearance in a match. It is never a hero.
9. A callback must change meaning on reread. Grey's final final v3 is an ability memo, Howard's filing date and Forever's infinite loop by the third appearance.
10. A chain beat is content-gated, not asset-promoted. If a Receipt, Repost or Camera beat needs a clip, prop or model, it becomes a `rule-demand` row; only a `ready` hy3d row with a real SHA-256 may be called `available`. See `plan/33`. A missing asset never invents a path, never changes Clout or a kit, and never makes `003` playable.
11. The three chains escalate exactly three times: introduce, misfile, appeal/wipe. The Receipt Chain ends with Howard dating the documents, the Repost Chain ends before vibe, and the Camera Chain ends with Shoop erasing the cameraman. Forever's repeating clip is the named mechanical exception, not a fourth joke beat.

12. A CDN name is extraction evidence, not a punchline prop. When wscheppity captures a generated-on-demand clip or texture with real bytes, the win is 100% upstream extraction; BOTA may cite the hash but may not put `/cdn/` into a shipped joke, kit, voice line or hero key until hy3d publishes a `ready` content row for that BOTA use. Zero-byte placeholders remain `spawn-generated`, so `Receipt pending` and `Fact check pending` stay funnier than a false `verified` caption.

13. A live extraction watcher is not an audience. `coordination-status.json` may say a CDN name has HTTP evidence or hash-verified bytes; no announcer line, recap glyph or chain beat may call that `verified` in-game. The Receipt Chain still bills the play, the Repost Chain still refuses to end, and the Camera Chain still asks whether the jaw is in frame.
