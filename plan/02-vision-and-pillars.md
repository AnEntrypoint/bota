# 2. Vision and pillars

Numbers in this chapter are UNVERIFIED.
6.88 Brainrot is a caricature MOBA. Every hero, item and creep is drawn to be read at a glance, and rewards a second look. The game runs on a classic MOBA decision set. The jokes ride on top of it.

The AI theme is satire of the market, not of a real product. The AI items (section 11) carry it.

Four rules every system must pass:

1. **Readable.** A player can tell what a unit or item does from its silhouette and color, without reading text.
2. **Decisive.** Each system creates a choice with a cost on each side.
3. **Mapped.** Each system holds the job of a classic system (table below). A system needs a classic job to earn its place.
4. **Off the read path.** Jokes live in tooltips, announcers, idle animations and marginalia. They never hide a gameplay signal.
