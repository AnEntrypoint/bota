# 12. Neutrals, boosts, The Trend and Snapshot
### Jungle camps

Numbers in this section are UNVERIFIED.

Camp types: small, medium, hard and elder. Elders are magic-resistant (not spell-immune).

| Creature | Camp type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background NPCs | Small | Repeat one voice line. Grey, not a team colour. |
| Spam | Small | Fast, low HP |
| Shill | Medium | Casts a slowing aura |
| Troll-Bridge | Hard | Blocks a bridge crossing until killed |
| Old Forum Ticker | Elder | Long-lived creature, magic-resistant |
| Static Imp, Reverb Wraith, Ticker Sprite, Bio Bot, Bullish Golem, Cancel Mob, Markdown Hound | Mixed | Stats by camp type, below |

Stacking:

- A camp respawns on the minute if its spawn box is empty.
- Pulling a camp out at about :53 to :55 stacks an extra camp for the next minute.
- Camp stat budgets: Small 300 HP, Medium 550, Hard 950, Elder 1,600, with damage rising 20 per tier (GUESS: 6.88g gives no step; the balance pass sets it; section 19 budgets cover heroes, not neutrals).
- Job: neutral camp reward (plan/02, Neutral items row). The cost is the camp fight.
- Stage camps: the camps beside the river's shallow crossings. A pull made while an enemy hero can see the camp gives +1 Clout per creature killed. Unwatched pulls give normal gold only.

### The Trend

- Job: boss objective (plan/02, Boss objective and revive reward row). The cost is the pit contest, or the towers traded for it.

A faceless boss built from stacked trending banners. Every number in this block is UNVERIFIED.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| HP | 8,000, plus 175 per minute |
| Magic resistance | 55% |
| Respawn | 8 to 11 min, random |
| Reward | Gold and XP. The killing team picks one of two rotating modifiers for 4 min. The Trend drops Gamer Fuel on its second kill and every kill after (UNVERIFIED). The killer takes the Snapshot. |
| Snapshot | One revive at the Spawn Lounge after a 3 s rewind clip. Expires 5 min after pickup. |

### Trend modifiers

Numbers in this section are UNVERIFIED.

- Job: patch meta (plan/02, Patch meta row). The cost is the modifier you do not take.

Eight modifiers rotate each patch. Two are offered per kill:

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Cast Rush | +10% move speed for 3 s after each cast. Each cast reveals the caster for 1 s. |
| Unboxing | −15% item cooldowns, items cost 10% more gold |
| Learn to Code | +10% XP from all sources, abilities cost 5% more mana |
| Speedrun | +15% move speed, towers deal +10% damage to you, and your team earns 10% less tower gold |
| Storytime | Channeled abilities finish 25% faster and cost 15% more mana |
| Get Ready With Me | Items bought at the Spawn Lounge cost 10% less. Respawn takes 2 s longer. |
| Demand Tide | Clout gain +20%, Clout decay +50% |
| Solo Queue | +8% ability damage while no ally is within 600, -10% move speed |

The modifier shows on the minimap timer as its own icon, one per modifier, and as a team-colored HUD filter for its duration.

### Breaking News (event)

Numbers in this section are UNVERIFIED.

- Job: capture objective (plan/02, Capture objective row). The cost is the contest, which stops progress while both teams have heroes inside.

Two events per match, at 18:00 and 30:00 (UNVERIFIED). A banner slides in using the Trend banner effect, and a 60 s capture zone opens at the river center.

- **Capture:** a team with heroes inside and no enemy hero inside holds the zone. After 20 s held, its heroes get Front Page for 40 s: Reach +100.
- **Contest:** if both teams have heroes inside, no progress is made.

Front Page changes Reach only. It does not reveal anyone.

### Snapshot

The Snapshot is held by one hero. A hero holding it at the Spawn Lounge can revive once. The revive replays the death as a rewound clip. The Snapshot is the Trend's revive reward. The bought Participation Trophy (section 11) gives a revive too and sells under the general rule.
