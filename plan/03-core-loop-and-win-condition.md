# 3. Core loop and win condition

Numbers in this chapter are UNVERIFIED.
Two teams of five. Each team defends a base, a set of lanes, and an Exchange. Destroying the enemy Exchange wins the match.

### Lanes and structures

| Structure | Per side | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 tower | 3 (one per lane) | Armor aura for allied heroes: +1. Attack 60 damage (UNVERIFIED). |
| Tier 2 tower | 3 | Armor aura: +3. Attack 90 damage (UNVERIFIED). Invulnerable until its T1 falls. |
| Tier 3 tower | 3 | Armor aura: +3. Damage equal to T2. |
| Tier 4 towers | 2 (at the Exchange) | Invulnerable until a T3 falls in any lane |
| Content Mills | 6 (two per lane, melee and ranged) | A lane with both its Content Mills down spawns Mega creeps; a destroyed mill respawns after 5 min (UNVERIFIED) |
| All six Content Mills down | n/a | Mega creeps spawn in all three lanes until a mill respawns |
| Exchange | 1 | Invulnerable until both T4s fall. Destroying it wins (plan/02, Win objective row). |

- Job: map pressure, rotation (plan/02, Map pressure, rotation row). The cost is the tower held or the lane rotated away from.

- Tower HP: T1 1,300, T2 1,600, T3 1,600, T4 2,200. Gold per tower: the Tower row of plan/05.
- Towers regenerate and take reduced damage when no enemy creeps are within 900 range (UNVERIFIED: 2% max HP per second; damage taken halved) (backdoor protection).
- Job: base shield (plan/02, Base shield row). The cost is the 5 min cooldown, which makes the timing the choice.
- Go Private (padlock): makes all team buildings invulnerable for 5 s. Cooldown 5 min. It refreshes when a T1 of the caster's team falls.

### Creep waves

- Job: creep wave pressure (plan/02, Creep wave pressure row). The cost is the lane held.

- A wave spawns every 30 s: three melee and one ranged per lane.
- A siege creep joins every fifth wave.
- Creeps upgrade every 7.5 min: +12 HP and +2 gold per upgrade. Melee and ranged creeps upgrade; siege creeps do not. Four upgrades at most (GUESS: caps late creeps at 48 HP above base so waves stay killable; the balance pass sets it).
- Aggro: attacking an enemy hero draws creeps within 900 range for 2.5 s (UNVERIFIED), with a 2.5 s cooldown that starts when the draw ends.

### Match flow

1. Draft (section 14). Players spawn at the Spawn Lounge. Creeps spawn at 0:00.
2. Laning, 0:00 to about 10:00. Farm, deny, trade, ward.
3. Rotations and objectives. The Trend opens at about 10:00 (plan/05).
4. Pushing. Break T1s, then T2s and T3s, then content mills.
5. Exchange.

- Job: surrender (plan/02, Surrender row). The cost is the rest of the match given up.

Surrender rule: at 30 minutes, a team can vote to surrender with 4 of 5 players agreeing. After a failed vote, the next vote opens 300 s later (UNVERIFIED).
